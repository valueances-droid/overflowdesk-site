Example timeline. Times are illustrative.

Your phone works the same. The calls you miss get answered.

  1. 01

    You forward the calls you miss

    Your phone keeps ringing like it does today. When nobody picks up after a few rings, or the line is busy, your carrier forwards the call to your Overflow Desk line. Most business lines have this setting, and we walk you through it.

  2. 02

    Your receptionist answers

    It greets callers with your business name and works from a script we write with you: services, service area, hours, diagnostic fee, and what counts as an emergency. It doesn't make up prices or promise arrival times.

  3. 03

    You get the job

    When the call ends you get a summary and the recording by email, right away. The caller gets a text saying you have their info. Emergencies like a breaker that keeps tripping or a burning smell get transferred to whoever is on call.

FAQ

Questions owners ask.

Will callers know it's not a person?

Yes. It's an AI receptionist, and it never claims to be a person. It answers with your company name and tells callers it's an AI assistant. If a caller would rather talk to a person, it takes their info and your team calls them back.

Do I have to change my number?

No. You keep your number and your carrier. Only calls you don't answer get forwarded. Turn the forwarding off and everything is back to how it was.

What about real emergencies?

You tell us what counts as an emergency for your shop. Those calls get transferred straight to the on-call phone. If someone reports burning, smoke or sparks, it tells them to leave the house and call 911 first.

Will it quote prices?

Only the ones you give us, like a diagnostic or trip fee. It won't guess at repair or replacement prices. Those stay with your techs.

How long does setup take?

Call answering is live within 2 business days; text-back turns on within 2 weeks, once carrier registration clears. It starts with a 20-minute call about your services, towns, hours, fees and what counts as an emergency. Then we test it with you before you turn on forwarding.

What if it doesn't work for us?

Cancel by email any time. If it doesn't catch a single call you'd have missed in your first 30 days live, you get all of it back, setup and monthly.

Is there a contract or per-minute billing?

No contract, cancel anytime. The founding price is a flat $97 a month, open to the first 10 shops.

Does it speak Spanish?

English only for now. Spanish-speaking callers get an English assistant.

Is the call recorded?

Yes. The greeting tells callers the call is recorded. Recordings and summaries go to you. If you want one deleted, email us.

What happens to my customers' info?

It goes to you. We use it only to run your line, we don't sell it, and we delete it when you ask or when you cancel. The Privacy Policy has the details.
