Terms of Service

These terms cover your use of Overflow Desk, an AI receptionist service for HVAC, plumbing and electrical shops, run by Gabo as a small independent business. By signing up or paying, you agree to them. They're written in plain language on purpose.

What the service is

Overflow Desk gives your business a phone line that answers calls you forward to it. An AI receptionist greets callers with your business name, tells them they've reached an AI assistant and that the call is recorded, collects their details, texts them back, and sends you a summary and the recording by email right after the call. Call answering is live within 2 business days; text-back turns on within 2 weeks, once carrier registration clears. It speaks English only for now. It can transfer calls you define as emergencies to a phone number you choose.

What it isn't

  • It's not a person and not a call center. It can misunderstand callers, mishear details, or fail to answer if a phone, carrier or provider has an outage.
  • It's not an emergency service. It's not a replacement for 911 or for your own on-call process.
  • It doesn't schedule jobs, quote prices you haven't given us, or promise arrival times.

Setup

We set up your line within 2 business days of signup. Setup starts with a short call to go over your services, service area, hours, fees and emergency rules. You turn on call forwarding with your carrier after we've tested the line with you.

Pricing and billing

  • Founding price: $297 one-time setup, then $97 a month, with the first monthly charge 14 days after you sign up. The founding price is open to the first 10 shops and stays the same for as long as you remain a client.
  • Payments are processed by Stripe.
  • No contract. You can cancel any time by emailing us. Cancelling stops future monthly charges.

30-day guarantee

If it doesn't catch a single call you'd have missed in your first 30 days live, you get all of it back, setup and monthly. Email us within that window to ask.

Your responsibilities

  • Give us accurate information for your call script and tell us when it changes.
  • Follow the laws that apply to your business, including any rules about recording calls and texting customers in your state.
  • Don't use the service for anything illegal, deceptive or abusive.

Limits

We work hard to keep the service running and accurate, but we provide it as is, without guarantees beyond the refund described above. To the extent the law allows, we aren't responsible for lost jobs, lost profits or other indirect losses, and our total responsibility for any claim is limited to what you paid us in the 3 months before the claim.

Changes and ending the service
