Privacy Policy

Overflow Desk is a small independent business run by Gabo. We answer missed calls for HVAC, plumbing and electrical shops ("our clients") with an AI receptionist. This page explains, in plain words, what information we handle and what we do with it.

Who this covers

  • Our clients: the shop owners who sign up.
  • Callers: people who call a client's business and reach the Overflow Desk line.
  • Visitors: people who read this website.

What we collect

  • From callers: the phone number they call from, what they tell the AI receptionist (for example name, address and the problem), a recording of the call, and a written summary.
  • From clients: business name, contact details, the details we need to write your call script (services, service area, hours, fees, emergency rules), and billing records. Card payments are handled by Stripe; we don't see or store full card numbers.
  • From website visitors: nothing beyond what our web host needs to serve the page. We don't add analytics, ad trackers or cookies to this site.

Call recording and the AI

Calls answered by Overflow Desk may be recorded. The AI receptionist tells callers at the start of the call that they've reached an AI assistant and that the call is recorded. It does not claim to be a person.

How we use it

  • To answer calls for our client, text the caller back about their request, and send the client a summary and the recording by email.
  • To set up, test and improve the client's call script.
  • To bill clients and talk to them about their account.

We don't sell personal information, and we don't use callers' information to market to them.

Who we share it with

Caller information goes to the client whose business was called. We also use a small number of service providers to run the service, such as our phone and text provider, our AI and transcription providers, our email provider, and Stripe for payments. They handle data only to provide their service to us. We may disclose information if the law requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep call recordings and summaries while the client's account is active so the client can refer back to them. When a client cancels, or asks us to delete something, we delete it from our systems within a reasonable time, unless we have to keep it for legal or billing reasons.

Your choices

Callers can ask the business they called, or us, to see or delete information about them. Clients can ask us for a copy of their data or to delete it. Email valueances@mail.grokbot.com and we'll respond.

Security

We take reasonable steps to protect the information we handle. No system is perfectly secure, so we can't promise that information will never be accessed without permission.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top of this page.
